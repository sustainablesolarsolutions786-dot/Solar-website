Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about going solar

What happens on cloudy/rainy days? Do panels work?

Yes! Solar panels work in cloudy weather. They generate 50-80% of normal power on overcast days. Plus, our net metering system credits extra electricity you generate on sunny days, which you can use anytime. You'll never run short of power.

Will solar panels damage my roof?

Absolutely not! We use non-penetrating, waterproof mounting systems. Your roof remains completely intact with no holes or damage. Our installation includes a proper weatherproofing seal that actually protects your roof further. We inspect your roof first to ensure it's suitable, and we guarantee zero damage.

Kya maintenance lagti hai? How much upkeep is needed?

Maintenance is minimal and virtually free! Just occasional panel cleaning (every 2-3 months) to remove dust and bird droppings. We provide completely free annual maintenance and inspections. The hybrid inverter requires no special maintenance. Solar panels have no moving parts, so they're extremely reliable.

How does the PM Surya Ghar Yojana subsidy work?

The government gives you direct cash subsidy up to ₹78,000 depending on your system size. We handle ALL paperwork completely free - applications, documentation, approvals, everything. The subsidy is transferred directly to your bank account. You save ₹30,000 (≤2kW), ₹60,000 (2-3kW), or ₹78,000 (3kW+). We make it 100% hassle-free.

EMI available hai? Can I pay in installments?

Yes! We offer flexible EMI options starting from just ₹2,999/month. We have partnerships with banks offering 0% interest financing for 60-84 months. The monthly savings from your solar system actually exceed the EMI amount, so you save money from Day 1! Talk to us for customized payment plans.

What if I sell my house later? What happens to the solar system?

Excellent question! Solar panels increase your property value by 4-5% (₹4-5 lakh for a ₹1 crore home). The buyer gets the benefit of the remaining warranty and free electricity. It's a major selling point. You can also transfer the warranty to the new owner. It increases your home's market appeal.

How long do solar panels actually last?

Solar panels come with a 25-year comprehensive warranty and typically last 30-40+ years! After 25 years, they still generate 80%+ of their original capacity. We use Tier-1 panels from Adani, Waaree, and Tata that have industry-leading durability and reliability. Your investment is protected long-term.

Kitne din mein installation hota hai? How long is the installation process?

Residential installations are completed in just 2-3 days! Commercial setups take 5-7 days depending on system size. Our expert team works efficiently with minimal disruption to your routine. We handle all electrical work, net metering paperwork, and testing. You can start generating electricity and saving money within a week!

Are your panels MNRE approved? What about certifications?

Yes! All our panels and systems are MNRE (Ministry of New and Renewable Energy) certified and approved. We're registered with all government programs. Our installers are certified professionals. We follow all building codes and electrical safety standards. Your system qualifies for government subsidies because of these certifications.
